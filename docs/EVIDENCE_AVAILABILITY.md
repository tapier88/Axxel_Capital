# Evidence availability

Scientific reports under reports/evidence and reports/validation retain their original
aggregate results, falsifications, manifests and hashes. Checks of those records do not
re-execute research or read VALIDATION/LOCKED_OOS datasets. Selected legacy store metadata
supports deny-before-payload tests; the referenced datasets are deliberately absent.

The canonical ADR and historical scientific corpus may refer to local reports/data,
models or exports. Excluded paths are preserved references, not missing implementations:
reports/data_integrity, reports/foundation_recovery_plan, reports/track_b_xm_forward_capture_v1,
reports/track_b_xm_forward_capture_v2 and reports/master_architecture_exports are local-only.
Their status is summarized in PROJECT_STATUS.md and FLOW_AUDIT.md. The original data
integrity registry has host paths and remains local; public status is a separate view.

V1 evidence is not redacted in place, overwritten or reinterpreted. V2 readiness remains
blocked; an unpublished host preregistration hash in canonical state refers to local
custody, not start approval. Historical document counts are scoped to their dates.
